WHAT YOU CAN REQUEST

Every certification and clearance issued by the barangay, now available through one online request form.

01
Barangay Certification / Clearance

General-purpose proof of residency for employment, school, or transactions.

02
Senior Citizen Certification

For Senior Citizen ID application (OSCA).

03
PWD Certification

For Person with Disability ID application.

04
Solo Parent Certification

For Solo Parent ID under RA 11861.

05
Certificate of Indigency

For medical, educational, legal, or burial assistance.

06
Certificate to File Action (CFA)

Required before filing a case in court.

07
Certificate to Bar Action (CBA)

Bars complainant from filing the same action.

08
Certificate to Bar Counter-Claim (CBC)

Bars respondent from filing a counter-claim.

09
Barangay Protection Order (BPO)

Emergency protection under RA 9262.

10
Facilities & Properties Request

Reserve barangay halls, courts, or equipment.
